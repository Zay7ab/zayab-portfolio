09 / HOW I WORK

Four steps, no three-month branches

01

Scope against the data

Before any model, I look at what you actually have. Most AI projects fail on data quality, not architecture, so this is where I tell you if the idea needs reshaping.

02

Thin slice to production

One narrow path working end to end — ingest, model, API, deployed — inside the first two weeks. You get something real to react to instead of a status update.

03

Evaluate, then widen

Accuracy, latency and failure cases measured on a held-out set. Only once the numbers hold do I broaden scope, so the thing never silently regresses.

04

Hand over, not hand off

Containers, CI, monitoring and docs, plus a walkthrough with whoever maintains it. You should not need me to keep it running.

15 / FAQ

Questions I get before the first call

ASK ME A QUESTION

If yours is not here, write — I answer within a day.

Do you take whole projects or join a team?

Both. Solo, I take an AI product from data to deployed API over roughly four to eight weeks. On a team I usually own the AI layer — retrieval, model serving, evaluation — and plug into your existing backend.

What if our stack is legacy PHP or .NET?

Fine. The AI service runs as its own containerised FastAPI process and talks to your app over HTTP. Nothing in your codebase has to move for the first version.

How do you price?

Fixed scope where the scope is clear — $199 for a proof of concept, $499 for a production build. Anything larger is quoted after a scoping call, or billed at $25/hr.

Which time zones do you work with?

I am in Karachi (UTC+5). That overlaps comfortably with the Gulf, Europe and the UK, and I keep early mornings open for US clients.

Can you prove the accuracy numbers?

Yes. Every figure on this page comes from an evaluation I can walk you through — test sets, confusion matrices, retrieval relevance scores and the load-test output.
